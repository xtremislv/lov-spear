Lov-Spear Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026 · applies to Lov-Spear 1.3 and later

Lov-Spear converts and edits files on your PC and opens a launcher wheel for your apps. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no telemetry. We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data.

Your files

Every conversion and tool runs locally on your computer with the engines bundled in the app (FFmpeg, 7-Zip and Python). Your files are never uploaded. New files are saved next to the originals or in the folder you choose.

Data stored on your PC

  • Settings, logs and your launcher apps and workspaces are stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Lov-Spear (inside the app's private storage when installed from the Microsoft Store).
  • Logs record app events (for example "conversion finished") to help with troubleshooting. They stay on your PC and are never sent anywhere.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the program; you can delete the folder above to remove your settings too.

Mouse and keyboard

To open its wheels, Lov-Spear watches for its gestures (Shift while dragging files, and Shift + holding the right mouse button). This happens only in memory on your PC. Keystrokes and mouse movements are not recorded or stored.

Internet access

The file tools work fully offline. The only time Lov-Spear connects to the internet is when you add a website shortcut to the launcher: it then downloads that website's icon (favicon) from public icon services (unavatar.io, with Google's favicon service as a fallback). Only the website's domain name is sent. Opening a website shortcut opens it in your browser, which is governed by that browser's own privacy policy.

Children

Lov-Spear does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Open source

Lov-Spear is built on ZestDrop (MIT licence) and Rovyl (GNU GPL v3.0). Its source code changes are published in the source folder of this website's repository.
